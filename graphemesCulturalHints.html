Chinese idiosyncrasy and history through its graphemes

Some graphemes open a cultural window onto past practices and values, as these show:

  • 取 A hand 又 taking the ear 耳 of a fallen enemy. In ancient China the ears of enemies were taken as war trophies. Related: 馘 To cut off 或 the enemy's ears 首.
  • 卜 or ⺊, a pictogram of a crack in an oracle bone. These bones were thrown into the fire for divination (pyromancy), reading the pattern of the cracks the fire left behind. Read more on Wikipedia We also find 兆 omen and 爻 diagrams for divination. They compose with 口 mouth: 占 to divine; to observe; to versify and 卟 a chemical compound; divination; to consider. Also 卦 divination, fortune-telling or 拈 to pick up with the fingers; to draw lots.
  • 貝 sea shell; money, currency. Certain shells were used as currency in ancient China. Wiktionary
  • 示 altar; ceremony; to show, to demonstrate is a pictogram showing a stone table for sacrificial offerings. Related: 祝 to pray; to wish well; surname, which shows a person 兄 before an altar 礻.
  • 娈 lovely, beautiful; docile, obedient. It expresses values attached to women in a patriarchal society.
